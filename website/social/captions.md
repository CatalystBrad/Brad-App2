### Posting tips
- **Instagram:** post the square (1080×1080) image, paste the IG caption. First comment is a great place to drop extra hashtags if you want a cleaner caption.
- **Facebook:** the same square image works fine; FB captions read best a little longer and conversational, as above.
- **Best times** (UK leisure/travel audience): weekday evenings 6–9pm and weekend mornings tend to perform well.
- Swap the placeholder website/phone for your real details before publishing.
