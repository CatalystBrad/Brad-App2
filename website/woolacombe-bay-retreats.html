The Area

Woolacombe & beyond

Consistently voted one of Britain's best beaches, Woolacombe's vast golden sands are a paradise for surfers, families and dog-walkers alike. The South West Coast Path winds along dramatic cliffs to Mortehoe, Croyde and Saunton.

  • Woolacombe Beach — 3 miles of Blue Flag sand, 5 min walk
  • Mortehoe Village — charming pubs & coast path, 1 mile
  • Surfing & paddleboarding — lessons & hire on the beach
  • Croyde & Barnstaple — surf town & market town nearby
